How this estimate works

Total cost = purchase price + energy expense + annual maintenance/insurance − estimated resale value. EV consumption is entered per 100 km and should include charging losses.

This version excludes loan interest/EMI, fuel-price inflation, charger installation, battery replacement, battery degradation and exceptional repairs. Add these separately when they are material. The saved comparison stays in this browser on this device; it is not cloud-synced.
